About Me

Building things that
actually matter

I'm Washim Shaikh, a Computer Science Engineering student with a habit of turning ideas into working systems. Over the past couple of years, I've worked across the full stack — from designing backend logic in Python and Django to crafting clean, responsive frontends — while spending a serious chunk of my time exploring how machine learning can solve real problems in agriculture, finance, and everyday life.

My projects aren't experiments that live in notebooks. AgriTrade, my major project, connects farmers directly with buyers through an e-auction platform — removing middlemen and giving farmers better control over pricing. I've also built a fraud detection system tailored for Indian transaction patterns, a face recognition attendance system, deep learning models for medical imaging tasks like brain tumor classification and driver drowsiness detection, and a personal finance tracker (FinTrackAI). Each of these came from a simple question: what would actually help someone here?

Through internships at Coincent (AI with Python), Yhills (Machine Learning), 1Stop (Full Stack Development), and currently iStudio (AWS — ongoing), I've learned one thing clearly — the gap between knowing a technology and using it well is closed through building. That's what I keep doing. If a problem is interesting and the solution could be meaningful, I'm already thinking about the architecture.
